## 7. Longer-term portfolio and strategy lens

-   **Equities:** Prefer disciplined, diversified exposure over reacting
    to a single-session index move. Watch earnings delivery, valuations
    and breadth.
-   **Banking:** Friday's leadership is constructive, but confirm with
    follow-through, asset-quality trends, deposit costs and credit
    growth.
-   **Gold:** Retain a strategic rather than purely tactical framework;
    reassess allocation against portfolio objectives and risk tolerance
    after sharp volatility.
-   **Crude-sensitive exposures:** Track energy costs, inflation
    sensitivity and currency effects when evaluating sectors and
    companies.
-   **Liquidity and risk:** Keep position sizing and portfolio
    concentration aligned with volatility. Avoid treating derivatives
    positioning as a substitute for fundamental analysis.
